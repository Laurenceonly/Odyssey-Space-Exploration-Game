# Planet texture credits

The `2k_*.jpg` equirectangular maps are by Solar System Scope / INOVE:
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source pack uses NASA imagery and elevation data, with reconstructed gaps
and adjusted colors. These are visualization maps, not unmodified spacecraft
photographs. Files are unchanged; lighting, clouds, night-light masking and
subtle relief are applied by the renderer. The procedural comet and asteroid
surfaces are original to this project.

The Mercury map was retrieved from the same author's Wikimedia Commons copy:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_2k_mercury.jpg
