FLIGHT MANUAL / 01

Your ship is ready.

Use your keyboard to fly, steer, boost, and scan. During manual flight or after an autopilot arrival, choose Walk inside beside the camera views, or press I. The ship stops and holds position while you explore. Use WASD to walk and mouse dragging or arrow keys to look. Walk through the open doorway to explore the cockpit. Press E near the pilot seat to take flight controls, or Escape to return to your flight view. After landing on a planet or the Moon, use WASD to walk, drag with the mouse to look, J or Space to jump, Shift to run, and C to switch camera views. Follow the trail to the local landmark and press F to record its discovery. Return to your parked ship and press E to board, then take off.
