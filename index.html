    FLIGHT MANUAL / 01

    Your ship is ready.

    Use your keyboard to fly, steer, boost, and scan.

    During manual flight or after an autopilot arrival, choose Walk inside beside the camera views, or press I. The ship stops and holds position while you explore. Use WASD to walk and mouse dragging or arrow keys to look. Walk through the open doorway to explore the cockpit. Press E near the pilot seat to take flight controls, or Escape to return to your flight view.

    After landing on a planet or the Moon, use WASD to walk, drag with the mouse to look, J or Space to jump, press Space again in the air for one suit boost, Shift to run, and C to switch camera views. Follow the trail to the local landmark and hold R near its survey flag to scan it. In water, swim normally with your head up, or hold Shift while moving for a faster face-down stroke. Running and fast swimming share stamina; walk, swim normally or rest to recover. Space and J do not jump while swimming. On Earth, press M or choose Map to see your position and explored ground. You can walk, run, and jump with the map open; it follows you until you pan, and the recenter control resumes following. Coordinate editing stops movement, while Settings pauses play. Follow routes to find closed portals for future Earth regions. Return to your parked ship and press E to board, then take off.
